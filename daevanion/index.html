      Your stats

      Without any Ariel nodes. The best route depends on these: 1.5% Damage Boost is worth more at 50% than at 200%.

      How it is calculated

      NCsoft has not published a formula. This is the community-tested one; both numbers are against a PvE boss.

      damage = ( [ ((Attack × Weapon Boost) + Attack Bonus) × Attack Increase
             + PvE Attack + Boss Attack ] × skill coefficient
           + skill flat damage − target Defense × 0.1 )
       × (1 + Damage Boost + PvE Boost + Boss Boost − target Tolerance)
       × crit × double × front/back
      • PvE and Boss Attack are added after Attack Increase, so each point is worth 1 ÷ your Attack.
      • All Damage Boost types add into one bucket. +1.5% on a bucket of 180% is 1.5 ÷ 180 = +0.83% damage. The more you have, the less each node gives.
      • Damage Tolerance takes away from the boss's bucket in the same way.
      • Defense removes a flat 0.1 × Defense from every hit.
      • Critical Hit: about 1% chance per 15 points, up to the cap. A crit does 1.5× plus your Critical Damage Boost, minus the boss's resist. At the cap, crit nodes are worth nothing.
      • HP adds HP ÷ your HP. Critical Hit Resist and MP are worth nothing against bosses, because bosses do not crit.
      • Accuracy and Evasion count for nothing unless you give them a value in Your stats.

      Effective HP is how much raw boss damage you survive: HP ÷ damage taken. Each node gets a value from these formulas; the planner then finds the connected group of nodes with the highest total value for your crystals.
