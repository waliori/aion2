How the route is found.

The goal is a single number. The formula under My stats gives the damage you deal and the hits you survive for any board. Damage maximises ln(damage), Survival maximises ln(effective HP), and Both maximises half of each.

The rules make it hard. Every node bought must touch one you already hold, back to the start, and the total cost must fit your crystals. Mathematicians call this a budgeted maximum-weight connected subgraph. No fast method is known that always solves it, so the page works in two stages.

  1. Quick search. Each node gets the gain it brings by itself. On a random spanning tree of the board, a knapsack over the tree finds the best connected board exactly. This is repeated on 300 to 1,500 trees, then once more with the gains measured again at mid-route. You see this route at once.
  2. Exact sweep. The board is read cell by cell. For every way the cells on the reading line can be taken and linked to each other, and for every number of crystals spent, only the best value is kept. A board is dropped only when a bound proves it cannot win.
  3. The real formula. Stats multiply, so the gains of two nodes do not simply add. The curve of each stat is capped by a straight line. The sweep on those lines gives a ceiling that no board can pass, and the best real board found so far is the floor.
  4. Closing the gap. The lines are tightened and the ranges of stat totals are cut in two, until ceiling and floor meet. The page then says “Proven best”. If a minute passes first, it gives the margin that is left.

On the class boards the four boards are swept one after the other and the shared pool is split between them exactly. The buy order is not part of the proof: it takes the best gain per crystal first, always next to what you hold.
